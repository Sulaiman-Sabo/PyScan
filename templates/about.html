About This Project

PyScan was developed by SULAIMAN SABO from FEDERAL UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY, BABURA. as part of a BSc Cybersecurity Final Year Project at the Federal University of Technology, Babura (FUTB), Nigeria. The project demonstrates the practical application of deep learning techniques to software security, bridging the gap between academic research and real-world vulnerability detection.

The system is designed to be production-quality and fully functional, serving as both a research prototype and a deployable security tool.
